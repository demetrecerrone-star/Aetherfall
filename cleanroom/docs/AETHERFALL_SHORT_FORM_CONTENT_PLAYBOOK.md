# Aetherfall Short-Form Content Playbook

Notes distilled from the supplied transcript of Vladislav Sateev's "Master Viral 3D Short Form Content in Blender". These are marketing/content principles, not in-game modeling rules.

## Core structure

Every short should be built around:

1. Hook
2. Retention
3. Reward

Do not spend the opening explaining what the viewer is about to see. Start with the strongest visual or result immediately and explain while the video is already moving.

## Visual hooks

- Open with a striking result, transformation, reveal, unusual animation, boss, spell, town view, or before/after.
- When possible, start with the payoff and then show how we got there.
- Let the visuals communicate obvious conclusions instead of narrating every fact.
- Prefer movement, scale, contrast, and transformation over static exposition.

## Retention

- Keep shorts tightly paced.
- Use clear progression such as “3 steps”, “before / after”, or “from blockout to final”.
- Avoid dead middle sections; the middle must keep delivering new visual information.
- Loop the ending back into the beginning when it feels natural.
- For very short clips, design for rewatchability rather than just completion.

## Reward

The ending must deliver something worth staying for:
- completed character/outfit reveal
- monster or boss transformation
- town before/after
- building interior reveal
- animation test payoff
- gear equip sequence
- dramatic in-game shot

The viewer should feel the final moment justified the time spent watching.

## Consistent Aetherfall identity

Use a repeatable visual language:
- consistent title typography
- consistent logo placement
- consistent music family
- consistent color treatment
- recognizable pacing and edit rhythm
- repeatable opening/closing structure

Do not reinvent the presentation style for every post once a format is clearly working.

## Aetherfall content formats to test

- “We fixed the worst part of this armor.”
- “From blockout to anime RPG character in 15 seconds.”
- “This town used to be empty.”
- “Building a blacksmith players can actually enter.”
- “One outfit, two characters, zero clipping.”
- “Watch this peasant outfit go from broken to game-ready.”
- “We made this entire RPG asset ourselves.”
- “Before / after: Android build vs Blender source.”
- “Can an MMO town feel alive on a phone?”
- “The final 3 seconds show the full transformation.”

## Measurement

Treat retention as a creative diagnostic:
- weak opening retention -> change hook or opening visual
- drop in the middle -> compress or add a new visual beat
- strong completion but weak replay -> improve loop or ending-to-beginning connection
- strong replay -> reuse the format and vary the subject

Do not chase a single universal percentage threshold; compare each format against our own channel history.

## Blender production use

For marketing shots, Blender is a presentation tool:
- stage clean before/after comparisons
- use controlled camera motion
- use simple lighting that clearly reveals form
- render vertical framing from the start
- favor readable silhouettes over overly detailed shots
- build loops that can restart seamlessly

Final in-game credibility shots should still come from Godot/Android when the claim is about gameplay.


## Knowledge-backed idea generation

Use a repeatable creative brief rather than asking for random ideas every time.

For each Aetherfall short, generate:
- concept/title
- opening visual hook
- 15–25 second script
- scene-by-scene shot plan
- Blender/Godot asset requirements
- final payoff/reveal
- optional seamless-loop idea
- truth label: real development footage, dramatized concept, or fictional scenario

Before proposing ideas, consult the Aetherfall production docs and any legitimately licensed/reference materials we have permission to use. Do not rely on pirated books or unauthorized copies.

### Prompting rules

- Ask for multiple concepts at once, then rank them by hook strength, production difficulty, novelty, and relevance to current Aetherfall work.
- Require a complete ending; avoid unresolved hooks that frustrate the viewer.
- Prefer third-person narration for documentary-style development stories unless first-person fits the clip.
- Distinguish clearly between real events and fictional/imagined stories.
- If a concept requires a new Blender technique (water, destruction, cloth, particles, crowds, etc.), flag it as a learning opportunity and estimate the extra production work.
- Reuse strong formats instead of reinventing the entire structure for every post.

### Aetherfall-specific creative agent behavior

The idea system should know the current production state:
- male/female character pipeline
- modular gear
- starter town and future interiors
- NPC/social spaces
- quests and quest boards
- blacksmith/inn/guild/player hub plans
- Android performance constraints
- goal of creating our own assets

It should prioritize ideas that simultaneously market the game and showcase meaningful development progress.
